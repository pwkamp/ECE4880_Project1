# FW-04 — LCD and Buttons

Connect the ESP32 micro-USB/UART cable, close ESP-IDF Monitor, and leave the box continuously ON; this section does not include a restart or power-off check. When the runner asks for each sensor, press its physical button once within 30 seconds. Do not type a separate confirmation: the runner automatically detects the firmware's debounced button marker, correlates the matching LCD-render marker, verifies the backend state transition, and records the button-to-render interval. Visually verify numeric, OFF, DISCONNECTED, signed-temperature, average rendering, and normal indoor readability when prompted. The intended button-to-render software contribution must not exceed approximately 20 ms. Adjustable backlight control is outside this test and no backlight manipulation is requested.
