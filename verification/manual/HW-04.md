# HW-04 — Temperature Accuracy and Thermal Response

Capture both sensors and a reference at room temperature (pass target 22 °C ±4 °C) and in a properly prepared stirred ice-water bath (0 °C ±2 °C). For each sensor, time an observable rise after hand heating and a faster rise from safe nearby/brief soldering-iron heating. Preserve the complete CSV, reference identity/calibration information, setup photos, timestamps, and safety notes. Code/simulator results do not verify physical accuracy.
