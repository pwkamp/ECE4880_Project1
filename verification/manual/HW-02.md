# HW-02 — Mechanical Robustness

Human evidence is mandatory. Photograph and functionally test the baseline. Perform the approved workbench-to-floor enclosure drop, upside-down operation, connected-cable drop, ordinary handling, and temporary sensor immersion. Photograph after-test condition, inspect all retention/strain relief/connectors, reconnect any permitted detached cable, and repeat the functional smoke test. Record height, surface, cable detachments, damage, and operator initials. A simulated or visual-only inspection cannot pass this test.
