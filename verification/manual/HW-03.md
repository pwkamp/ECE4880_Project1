# HW-03 — Power Switch and Hot-Plug

Disconnect the ESP32 micro-USB/UART cable before starting and power the box only through its battery or intended switched supply. Disconnect/reconnect Sensor 1 and then Sensor 2 while powered; verify the other channel continues, the fault is reported, and each sensor restores its pre-disconnection ON/OFF state. Switch the third box OFF and verify local operation/current remote data cease; switch ON and verify automatic recovery. Backend, database, and current-data captures provide the machine evidence. Human cable/switch actions are required; UART evidence is deliberately excluded because USB power would invalidate the OFF state.
